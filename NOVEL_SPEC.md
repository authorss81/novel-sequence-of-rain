# Novel Specification

Title: The Sequence of Rain
Repository slug: novel-sequence-of-rain
Genre: scientific fantasy / weather mystery
Male lead: A weather researcher who predicts spells by sacrificing forecasts
Length target: 580 chapters
Relationship policy: One slow-burn relationship or no romance, decided in the bible phase.
Power rule: Growth must be earned through practice, cost, failure, relationships, and changed decisions. The lead must not become instantly overpowered unless the approved genre premise requires it.

## Premise
A weather researcher discovers that spells obey a sequence hidden inside storms, but every prediction removes one real possibility.

## Ending
The weather system becomes accountable to the people who live inside its forecasts.
