# Volume 02, Batch 0004 — ACT IV, *The Ledger* (Chapters 83–96)

**Scope:** Fourteen chapters. Last batch of Volume 02; closes the volume. Calendar: Drownmonth has 30 days (Tue–Wed); batch runs Miremonth 1 (Thursday) into the third week of Miremonth. Miremonth: 30 days, begins Thursday. Third back-cut of the season: morning of the 11th of Miremonth, smallest of the three. No Gregorian month names. Elapsed spans as dates only.

**New month:** MIREMONTH — named in Chapter 83, once, in a character's mouth, plainly, without joke. Record length (30 days) and weekday column (1st = Thursday) in continuity; keep prose and table in agreement.

**Bindings/panels:** Exactly one binding and one panel in this batch (Chapter 87), sponsored by a district, attested by a reader who is not Marek. No second tide claim. Tide arithmetic is used as method (districts acting without a reader), not as a new claim.

Clause four (names what closed, never who) defeats its author correctly, in a room, with a clerk. Arithmetic note on page once: 7/6 against 1/4 is five and a half times. Ledger pays one claimant (ninth ward) and refuses another (Dellow — not a district, cannot be claimant) in the same afternoon.
