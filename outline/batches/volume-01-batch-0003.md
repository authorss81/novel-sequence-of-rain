## Batch goal

The method Marek named in Chapter 21 is now the official method, the Office has stopped using him, and the city has eleven days in which the body of the Bellweather Front is still arriving. This is the batch in which he takes a good claim and makes a good result, and in which the last comfortable thing he had — the belief that an honest man in a room could fix it — is taken away from him by a procedure that works.

**The batch cliff is Chapter 35, and it is not the storm.** The storm's worst has already happened by Chapter 32. Chapter 35 is a room with a ledger in it, a man who reclassifies a spend as maintenance failure because the spend happened on his watch, a twenty-seven-year-old clerk who will not write the false entry, and a Director whose authorization stands because nothing anybody did was against the rules.

## Starting condition

Day twenty. The Bellweather collision passed over the basin on the ninth day at about eighteen hundred and the sustained body of the same front is still in the basin and still arriving. A Council-certified trial claim is in force with a release at 04:40 on the twenty-first. Marek has said in public what a certified claim costs and cannot prove a syllable of it, and the minute that says so is on its way to nine watershed seats and three regional seats.

Marek holds: one private sequence plate in a tube, a coat cuff with two readings and a filing mark in three blocks of space, a bench sheet in a flat drawer, a chart margin with a piece cut out of it, a spare plate in a box in a tower, a brass surround around a temporary, two unfiled tags, an unfiled tag for a March dropout that is now on the public record as a fault with a cause, a bent bracket off a third hook in Low Spindle, a document he has told a woman is not ready, and a twelve-year-old date in a locked room that nobody knows he has.

**He is off the field list.** He is not on any watch, not on any roster, and not the man anybody will send for.

## Clock convention for this batch

A cycle is six hours; the watch bell is the hour. The weather phases in this volume are the **leading rain** (day seven), the **second belt** (the small hours of day eight), the **collision** (about 18:00 on day nine), and — from this batch — the **body** (the sustained western draw and the peak rain, days twenty-four to thirty-two). *Turn* remains the technical term for a single change in a sequence.

**No absolute hours-to-arrival may be stated anywhere in this batch.** The front's approach is described by clock time and day, and by what is on the instruments this morning. Do not re-derive an arrival from a chapter clock, and do not reintroduce the Office's sliding published figure: that instrument is finished as a clock, because Chapter 11 showed the published hour was approximately right and Chapter 12 showed it was useless for the thing anyone needed.

### Chapter 00022

**POV:** Nell Cray, Ferrygate district clerk.

**Location/time:** Ferrygate, day twenty-one, from about half past three in the morning. The Quay Steps, the low side, the market, the landing.

**Immediate goal:** Get the district through a release that has been certified to the minute, and go home.

**Resistance:** The claim is true. The market opens. The low side drains clear to the second watch, exactly as certified, and the factor's eleven carts and two barrows go down a certified road at first light with a claim behind them, and a Nacre Holds grain convoy comes through on the 04:40 to 08:00 window with four chartered barges. **Everything the claim said would happen happens.** The resistance is the third thing, which nobody told her about and which the claim did not name: the rain-garden rota is gone because the district withdrew it, and the ferry's extended hours are off the board because the district cancelled them, and at about five in the morning water comes through the door of number four on the Quay Steps and there is no rota anywhere in Veyra that says two people ought to be somewhere else.

**Information revealed:** **the nine hours.** Ottilie Fisk, seventy-seven, and Abel Fisk, eighty-two, ground floor, no stairs, are in a ground-floor room from about five in the morning until about two in the afternoon with water in it. The shelter rota that would have moved them is a two-line sheet in the district book and the district book says nothing, because a rota is not a plan any more once the plan it belonged to is not needed. The district improvises. **Nobody is hurt.** Nobody ever returns to that flat.

**Action or decision:** She finds the sheet herself, in her own book, in her own hand, and cannot produce a copy of it, because the copy is the original and the original is a piece of paper and paper does not carry a reason. She writes it out again, and the copy has no authority in it whatsoever. **The chapter must not make this a lament and must not make it a scene of rescued old people. It is a small administrative catastrophe and it is played as one.**

**Emotional change:** Not grief and not vindication. **The specific exhaustion of a competent clerk discovering that her own correct paperwork has become the mechanism, and that being right was never the same as being enough.** She is not a reader and she has no instrument and she spends nothing.

**Power or resource change:** **No spend. The nine hours is the tail of the Chapter 21 compound branch and is not a new binding.** What the district spends is a ground-floor flat, the contents of it, and an afternoon.

**Continuity fact:** The claim is entirely accurate and entirely discharged. The market opens on schedule and the grain convoy makes its window. **The rota sheet cannot be produced and no notice in Veyra covers any of it.** The two residents are named and individuated and nobody is hurt.

**Ending type:** A decision, and it is small. She files the re-copied rota, and this time she copies it four times, and sends one to the Warden's council, and knows exactly what she is doing and cannot stop doing it.

**Next-chapter pull:** The Council's report on the trial claim reaches nine watershed seats and three regional seats on the twenty-second, and it is very good, and the man who made it true in public is not sent for.

### Chapter 00028

**POV:** Marek Vale. **Batch midpoint.**

**Location/time:** The Weatherglass and the ward ledger bench, then Kestrel Yards, day twenty-eight.
