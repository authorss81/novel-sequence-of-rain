# Continuity State

## Canon status

Bootstrap design canon is established; chapter-level canon has not yet been dramatized. Treat the bible and series outline as the source of truth and reveal information gradually in prose.

## Fixed world rules

- Atmospheric spells move through the Six Movements: Gather, Weight, Break, Route, Release, Witness.
- A read observes without spending. A binding claim has a place, a time or trigger, an effect, and a witness; it can close one real branch in this world.
- A false binding still spends and causes a counterturn. A spent branch cannot be reopened, though a hidden or administratively sealed route may be distinct from a spent branch.
- Branches are not parallel universes. Counter-memory is evidence of absence, not a supernatural recovery mechanism.
- The Meridian began as a civic instrument for displaying disagreement. Later legal authority made one official claim binding across the basin.
- Rook signed the Meridian Cut order. Anja closed one branch to protect Low Spindle and died; the surviving Ninth Ward corridor was sealed and erased from the public record by Rook’s order, carried out by Ione Sere.
- The pre-Meridian origin of atmospheric sequence remains intentionally unanswered.

## Starting continuity

- Marek Vale, 32, is a Veyran Weather Office researcher and instrument repairer. He is a Reader, not a Conductor, and begins without a bound Bellweather claim in prose.
- Tarin Senn, 30, captains the independent Kestrel Line. Marek and Tarin have prior professional friction from the Hearthward rescue and no settled romance.
- Neris Kade is a Low Spindle Storm Warden. Sable Orin is a counter-reader. Ione Sere is the archive custodian. Cassian Rook is the final human antagonist.
- The first ten chapters must establish ordinary work, the Bellweather crisis, the read/binding distinction, a first spend, a first cost, and the Ninth Ward clue.

## Ending constraints

- The final conflict is the White Noon and Rook’s Last Forecast, not a new enemy.
- Marek refuses a single answer built on Anja’s sealed corridor, accepts incomplete information, and loses the ability to read a sequence alone.
- Twelve people die in the final operation; the loss is visible and compensated where possible, not erased.
- Rook survives, loses authority, and faces public judgment. Anja remains dead. Marek and Tarin share a home and workshop while retaining independent careers and the right to disagree.
