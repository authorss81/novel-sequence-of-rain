## Mystery threads

- **Bellweather spend:** **not yet opened in prose.** Planned for Chapters 27–35: Marek binds a Kestrel-corridor claim to save airships and loses the available repair-convoy branch for the Lower Sill culvert. The south-bank landslide is ordinary storm damage; the visible cost is the missing repair opportunity, not the landslide. **Chapter 13 has now set the convoy up and it is loaded and does not move; the mechanism that keeps it from moving — that a reach-level window is not a weather window, and the work is classified as maintenance, so nobody ever has to decide anything — is now on the page. Do not stage its failure before Chapter 32 and do not let anybody say it will not go.**
- **The cut index:** **opened as a hole only.** Chapter 10 gives a signature (A. Vale), a violet filed mark, a partial sequence reference, and a hand-trimmed margin. Chapter 21 has Marek notice the resemblance and then **say in the room that the two are not the same kind of thing and he is not going to pretend they were** — a piece of paper with a piece cut off it by a hand, against a room full of people and a bell. **The touch is a refusal to equate them, which is safer for the reader than an equation, and it names no document, an order, a year, or a person. That is the only place the two are allowed to touch.** The reader has not been told what the removed piece covered, who cut it, or that a routing clerk was named. The remaining fragment reading **VASK, O., R-9** belongs to Chapter 47. The hand is identified in **Volume 05**; the reason is withheld until **Ione's Volume 11 testimony**. Do not invent a separate culprit, and do not let the Volume 01 hook resolve to Rook alone.
- **The unrepaired trace:** **opened in Chapter 18.** The ninth ward tower's own sequence plate — Office stock, the one Marek fitted on day six and Malle passed in nine minutes, back in the seat since 22:00 on day eight — carries, six nights back in the cold substrate, a **complete, instrumented Witness movement dated twelve years back**, arriving in the glass from below. **It yields a date and nothing else**: no name, no document, no order, no institution. Marek cannot account for how a working plate holds a twelve-year-old registration. **Chapter 18 states in the text that the two plates are not two witnesses**: they ran face to face in one holder off one feed, so whatever the substrate holds it holds in both, and the only difference between them is who owns which. That is the fact the date rides on and the reason he cannot get corroboration. He has **not taken the plate, not copied the trace, and not written it in the tower's book**, and he knows the cost of each refusal. **He is the only person alive who knows. The first person to ask him directly is the open question of the batch.** Do not let it be resolved before the archive, and do not let anyone in Chapters 22–35 learn it exists by accident of narration.
- **Ninth Ward Record:** **not mentioned in prose and must not be.** Anja's signed forecast. Proves the surviving corridor was sealed and hidden rather than spent. Missing until Volume 11.
- **Crown Basin sheet:** not mentioned. A separate document. Records the branch she closed, the route she failed to save, a seal tag number, and Rook's signed order with the low-ward projection omitted. Found in Volume 12.
- **Meridian Cut:** **not named in prose and must not be** until its planned volume. Rook's signed order and Ione's deletion surface gradually.
- **Original Compact:** not named. Nobody has gone near Crown Basin, and the volume does not go there.

## Character threads

- **Marek's flaw:** **performed three times now, deliberately.** Chapter 7: withheld a branch cost and answered Malle with procedure. Chapter 9: gave Anwen a records sentence. Chapter 18: three conscious refusals to take, copy, or record a thing he was entitled to look at. Chapter 21: he stood up in a room and made a motion he could not support, having watched himself do it. **Chapter 21 is the first time the flaw has cost him both audiences at once**, and the later public failure in Volume 07, in which hiding a branch costs Tarin's crew a safe route, is still ahead of him and must not be anticipated.

She has now written four conditions in chalk, gone on a sortie to take an hour with two sets of eyes on it, and said in public that the Line is a witness and not a resource. **She has still not asked him the second question, and the shape of the answer has changed.**

Date.* She knows she cannot remember asking the people she numbers. She is not sorry and she is reorganising. Do not let Volume 03 name this before it happens.
