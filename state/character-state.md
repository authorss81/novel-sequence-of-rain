# Character State

Where the cast stands at the end of the most recently completed batch. For planned arcs, see `bible/characters.md`. For who is *written* so far, see `state/chapter-summaries.md`.

## Marek Vale

**Stage:** Reader. Unchanged. Has not followed a sequence across two towers and must not do so in Volume 01.

**Where he is at the end of Batch 0001:** walking down the hill to the low ground, at about half past five on the ninth day, half an hour before the front he has spent a week learning to see. Named *reader* on the Bellweather watch — low ground, ninth ward, second belt through the fourth watch — and walking down the hill to it. Carries a tube with two plates off one feed, four items in pencil on the inside of his left cuff, and a broom.

**What he has done:** Fitted a feed plate, scored and broken the poorest ward's unpaid replacement pane (serial 4486) and cut a private plate out of its crown in front of a glassblower who told him not to, made a binding claim he was not equipped to make, spent a branch without knowing it, reconstructed the spend alone, filed nothing, lied to his supervisor with a true-sounding mechanism, and signed his name to a record of the loss in a margin that has been cut open.

**Physical state:** Days without proper sleep. Inner-ear pressure half a tone up on the left, a metallic taste that began with the Chapter 5 binding and has not gone, a bent needle he does not remember picking up, a bench scar, and fine sound thin in the left ear in a way he attributes to the lamp. The Chapter 1 read cost him a headache, a ruined plate and a bent needle, and nothing else; the metallic taste and the inability to stop looking at a movement that has already stopped belong to the binding, not to the read. **This ear is not healed in this volume and the damage that does not heal arrives in Chapter 31.**

**Flaw, as performed rather than described:** He narrows a frightening problem until it becomes a procedure he can manage. He told Anwen Sarr about records procedure when she asked what her district gave up. He told Malle about rainfall when Malle asked what the claim ate. He noticed and set aside, in order, three separate moments when he failed to ask a question. The self-image and the behaviour came apart with nobody watching.

**What he owes:** Pell two years of unpaid tower glasswork. The Ninth Ward a public pane. Anwen Sarr a document, in writing, with his name at the top, publishing what a claim costs. Malle a sequence fault he has minuted and cannot prove. The low ground a reader who is going to say numbers out loud to strangers at eight o'clock.

**What he believes and should not:** He believes the Office is hiding something, and he is right, and he has just used the same machinery to hide something of his own, and he noticed the symmetry at the chart margin and did not do anything about it.

## Tarin Senn

**Where she is:** At Hearthward, on day eight morning, having lost a charter worth a month of the *Petrel*'s overhead on day five and having been the only person in the district who understood what the flood was before anybody did.

**State:** Has watched an accurate claim fail anyway, and cannot get from there to a mechanism. She asked the wrong question — who stood the crew down — and knows it was the wrong one. Angry at a habit rather than a person, which is the durable kind. Her regard for the Weather Office is now formally, publicly, and on a board, on the record.

**What she has of Marek:** One question asked and one half-answer given, two years after Hearthward. Competence acknowledged on both sides. No warmth and no coldness, which is its own kind of attention.

## Tovan Iles

**Stage:** One examination from a glass-tender's post. Has now written the first emergency-ledger entry in four years and the first weather claim of her career.

**State:** Terrified in the specific and reasonable way — she is the person whose handwriting turns out to matter. She filed Marek's reading in the honest drawer and put his name on the tab, which is the first of the acts that later cost her. She carries a faint, unexamined disappointment at a competent adult choosing a safe sentence. She has never been told what a claim is in the abstract; she learned it at a bench, as a job, from a man who needed her.

## Neris Kade

**Where she is:** Low Spindle, the lowest four blocks, the day before the second belt, with four crews, two pump houses, a school emptied at her own cost, and no readable public notice.

**State:** Anger redirected from a person to a habit, which is harder to indict and much more expensive. Her regard for Marek is exactly neutral, and she has written his name and the question *did he know* in her own notebook and given it no answer. She has accepted, in writing and against her own judgement, the duty of keeping a public list of what the notices do not say, and she has told Marek she will send it to his Deputy. She has an obligation to Sable Orin and no trust in her.

**Open in her:** The shelter rota that changed underneath her last winter and that nobody can reconstruct. It is the fifth line and she has not written it. She knows why she has not.

## Corrin Malle

**State:** On his feet since four the previous morning with four hundred and ninety hours to reach. He has asked Marek two questions — *what would prove you right*, and *what did the claim displace* — and been given a true answer and a circle. He has minuted the sequence fault without comment. He has put Marek on the Bellweather watch as a staffing solution and knows that is exactly what it is. He has not yet decided anything about the Hearthward entry, and a spend on his watch is a spend with his name on it.

## Lior Venn

**State:** Reclassified Sable Orin's corridor reservation at 14:10 on day nine under Rule 6, correctly, in a small level hand, and appended a sentence that will be quoted against him in Volume 02. He is the Office's **records** problem and has never watched a branch close. He does not argue. He answers.

## Pell Ardan

**State:** Holds the balance on a pane that is no longer a pane, and Marek's two years of tower glasswork, and Marek's knowledge that Pell knew exactly what was being asked and said it. He warned Marek that a ward with no public glass finds out from him eventually. Has not been told yet.

## Anwen Sarr

**State:** Accepted the condition, got the ruin, came up the hill with a clerk and a foreman and a bootlace-tied bundle of paper, asked the correct question, was given a sentence about procedure, and walked out carrying it. She has told Marek she will hold him in writing to publishing the cost of a claim. She has also said, out loud, in a public hall, that she was refused four forecasts in six years and that the forecasts are good and that the folding is the problem. **She is right, and the volume must not soften her.**

## Ferren

**State:** At the bottom of Hearthward's lowest row with a barrow he did not use. Says he has no memory of the night he did not work, that nobody stood him down, and that he cannot get past the fact that nothing went wrong and he was glad. Not lying. Counter-memory, first appearance, delivered to a stranger on purpose.
